Talk to CalculaMargem

CalculaMargem is maintained by a small team dedicated to building free tools and useful content for entrepreneurs. If you found something that could be improved, we'd love to hear about it.

Error in a formula or calculation

Found a result that looks wrong? Tell us the product, the values used and what result you expected. We check and fix it as fast as possible.

Suggestion for a new calculator

Missing a tool that would make a difference for your business? Tell us which one and why — the best suggestions become new features.

Question about pricing

Have a question about margin, markup, pricing strategy or break-even point? Check the site's content or send us your question.

Partnerships and collaborations

Interested in collaborating on educational content about finance for small businesses? Get in touch and let's talk.
