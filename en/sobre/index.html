What is CalculaMargem?

CalculaMargem is a portal of free tools built for entrepreneurs, shop owners, marketplace sellers and small businesses who need to accurately calculate the profit margin, markup and selling price of their products.

Created with a simple goal: make pricing knowledge accessible — knowledge that's often buried in complex spreadsheets, paid courses or technical finance books.

Our mission: Anyone — whether a first-time seller on an online marketplace or a business owner with years of experience — should be able to calculate their profit margin quickly, correctly and for free.

Why we built CalculaMargem

One of the most common mistakes in retail is confusing profit margin with markup. Many business owners apply a 30% markup believing they'll end up with a 30% margin — and only realize the problem once the books close in the red.

On top of that, anyone selling on marketplaces faces a maze of fees, commissions and shipping costs that make pricing even more complex. A simple percentage calculator isn't enough for that scenario.

CalculaMargem was built to solve these problems with:

Practical tools

Interactive calculators that work instantly in your browser, with no sign-up or installation required.

Educational content

Guides, real examples by industry, explained formulas and a complete FAQ on pricing.

Real-world context

Examples with real marketplace fees and practical local tax and cost considerations sellers actually face.

Free, with no barriers

No sign-up, no subscription, no usage limits. Use it as many times as you need.
